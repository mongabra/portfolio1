About

Grounded in numbers, drawn to building things.

I like problems that have a right answer hiding in the data — and I like building the tool that finds it.

I’m a third-year Bachelor of Economics and Statistics student at the University of Nairobi, with core coursework in probability & statistics, econometrics, mathematical economics, and quantitative research. Alongside my degree, I’ve trained in AI for Software Engineering and Data Analysis, and I work comfortably across Python, JavaScript, R, Advanced Excel, and SPSS.

Outside the classroom, I lead. I’ve served as Deputy Head of Clubs & Societies at Nairobi School, coordinated volunteers with the Red Cross, and currently work as a Sustainable Development Goals Campus Ambassador with AIIDEV Africa — running community sensitizations on the 17 SDGs, with a focus on decent work and economic growth.

I’m especially interested in responsible AI adoption and in using it to make markets — like labour markets — a little fairer.

MalipoHaki

An AI-powered wage fairness platform for Kenyan workers. Enter a job title, education level, years of experience, and location, and MalipoHaki benchmarks the wage against a market prediction — flagging when pay sits below the fair market average and by how much, so workers have real numbers to negotiate with.
